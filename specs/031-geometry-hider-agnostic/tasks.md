## Standing rules (apply to every task)

**Builds and tests (marked [RUN])**:
- The implementer asks the coordinator before each [RUN], then runs it through `~/.claude/bin/cpujob <label> <command>`: one job at a time machine-wide, `nice -n 19`, at most `-j4` on macOS; the determinism sweep and every Linux job run at `-j2`. A plain `cmake --build` / `ctest` line below always means the `cpujob` form, and so does every `cmake -B` configure (use the environment the coordinator gives; do not invent `VCPKG_ROOT`).
