# Master Baselines (T014)

**Feature**: 021-deferred-csg-evaluation | **Recorded**: 2026-10-01 | **Code**: master behaviour at `ff3eec0` (Phase 1 adds only empty stubs, the metric tool, the harness and scenes)

Produced by `tools/baseline.py` (raw results: `tools/baseline-master.json`; tables: `tools/baseline_report.py`). Scenes are generated by `tools/gen_scenes.py`, which also computes the analytic data in their headers.

## How these numbers were taken

- Apple M2 (arm64), Release build, one render at a time under `nice -n 15 taskpolicy -b` (the maintainer's thermal preference). Background priority stretches wall-clock time, so these are throttled times: compare them only against later runs taken the same way (T081).
- Each scene rendered once with `orender -x 3` and the visual-suite environment (`ORENDERHOME=build/tests/visual/_home`).
- The checks are the Phase 3 registrations' planned arguments, run directly with `test_csg_seam_metric` on these renders.

## Revised T034/T035 seam measurement (2026-10-03)

During Phase 3 (T051) the raytrace legs of T034's near seam and T035's curves stayed red on correct
renders: at a few angles the two operands shade to the same brightness (the hero's lit hole wall
against the sphere near 60° and 200°; the sphere's dark lower flank against the cavity face), so a
correct seam has no luminance step to detect. Some of master's "missing rays" below were this
effect, not seam spikes. With the maintainer's agreement the twelve scenes now give each operand
its own colour (hero: gold sphere, blue cylinder; sphere-cube: lavender sphere, orange cube) and
the checks measure colour steps (`--seam r:rgb`, `--edge-mode rgb`, new in the metric tool;
luminance stays the default for every other check). Master, re-measured with the revised checks:

## Spec 021 render times (T081, 2026-10-04)

Same machine and method as the master table above: `tools/baseline.py`, one render at a time under `nice -n 15 taskpolicy -b`, `orender -x 3` (raw results: `tools/baseline-021.json`). Background priority makes these throttled wall-clock times, so they are only comparable with the master column. Every ratio is under 10× (SC-007): the largest is 8.35×, the median 0.92×, and every render exited 0.

Two scenes were over the limit in a first run: `csg-coincident-difference-zbuffer` at 17.1× and `csg-coincident-difference-reyes` at 14.4×. A profile showed the cost was in solving the general bilinear patch-ray equation for every face of the coincident boxes; on a coincident face every REYES vertex is a boundary point. `countBilinearCrossings()` now takes a plane-and-parameters path for flat (parallelogram) quads. Flatness is judged against the corners' own float rounding (8·FLT_EPSILON × their magnitude), because the camera-space corners of a translated box are not exactly flat. Best-of-3 unthrottled times went from 0.58 s to 0.32 s (Z-buffer) and from 0.60 s to 0.34 s (REYES), against master's 0.07 s and 0.08 s. This table was taken after that change.
