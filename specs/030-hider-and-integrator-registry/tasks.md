## Departures from plan.md's phase outline (found by the tasks stage; each is for the maintainer to confirm at PAUSE 1)

## Standing rules (apply to every task)

**Builds and tests (marked [RUN])**:
- The implementer asks the coordinator before each [RUN], then runs it through `~/.claude/bin/cpujob <label> <command>`: one job at a time machine-wide, `nice -n 19`, at most `-j4` on macOS; the determinism sweep and every Linux job run at `-j2`.
