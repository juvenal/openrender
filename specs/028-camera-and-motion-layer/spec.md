# Feature Specification: Camera and Motion Layer

**Feature Branch**: `028-camera-and-motion-layer`
**Created**: 2026-10-27
**Status**: Clarified (2026-10-27), plan-stage decisions recorded (2026-10-27), plan approved with the maintainer's answers to Q1-Q8 (2026-10-27)
**Input**: Constitution Principle VIII remediation, layer L3, after spec 023 (GitHub #34). Motion blur and depth of field stop being implemented more than once. A shared camera layer (`ICamera::generateRay(x, y, t, lensUV)` and an immutable per-frame `FrameParams`) and one Geometry-owned motion evaluator (`atTime(t)` over the shutter-open and shutter-close keys) replace the raytrace hider's inline camera code and each primitive's own time interpolation. The raytrace hider and every primitive's intersection move onto them, one primitive family per phase; per-primitive reads of `CRenderer::invShutterTime` are removed. Moving Reyes onto the same layer is spec 031. Registry entry DB-009 (raytrace side); reduces DB-004 (#27) and DB-011 (frame parameters). Reference decisions: reference doc §5.4 (`ICameraService`) and §7.4 (BDPT camera importance, reserved) in `DEVNOTES_DETAILS/RENDER_ARCHITECTURE_REFERENCES.md`; spec 039's camera slots (film, lens, time).

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

The "users" of this feature are the people who build, maintain and extend openRender: contributors adding a hider or a primitive, maintainers reviewing Principle VIII remediation specs, the authors of specs 031 (Reyes onto the shared layers), 029/030 (hider separation and registry), 042/050 (direct and path-tracing integrators) and any later bidirectional integrator, and anyone rendering motion-blur or depth-of-field scenes, whose output must not change except through a change the maintainer approved (FR-014).

### Measured starting point (2026-10-27, master `4eb683b`)

All counts were taken on this tree with `grep` and a macOS Release build in an isolated build directory (`ctest -L architecture` passes: 21 of 21).

- **`invShutterTime` appears in 13 files, not only in Geometry.**
  - 7 Geometry files read it (24 reads): `quadrics.cpp` 14, `patches.cpp` 3, `polygons.cpp` 2, `curves.cpp` 2, `points.cpp`, `patchgrid.cpp` and `bsplinePatchgrid.cpp` 1 each. Each is a DB-004 link line in the allowlist (7 lines).
  - Every one of those 24 reads multiplies a position difference by `invShutterTime` to fill the `dPdtime` shading variable inside the primitive's `sample()`. **None is in an `intersect()`.** The brief's phrase "per-primitive `intersect` time interpolation reading `invShutterTime`" describes two separate things (see below).
  - Shading reads it twice in `shading.cpp` (the `time` and `dtime` range correction, lines 269 and 408) through the `CRendererServices::invShutterTime()` method (`rendererServices.h:65`), the services route spec 024 added. These are not allowlist lines.
  - The renderer core owns the static (`renderer.h`, `renderer.cpp:461-463`, `rendererStatics.cpp`) and implements the service method (`rendererServicesImpl.h:42`).
- **Per-primitive motion at intersection is a separate implementation, in 12 entry points plus 1.** Each interpolates its own motion on the ray's shutter time (`CRay::time`, normalized 0..1): the seven quadrics (`CSphere`, `CDisk`, `CCone`, `CParaboloid`, `CCylinder`, `CHyperboloid`, `CToroid`) interpolate their parameters and transform the ray through `transform()` (`objectMisc.h:178`); `CBilinearPatch` and `CPolygonTriangle`/`CPolygonQuad` interpolate their two vertex keys; `CImplicit` evaluates its function at the ray time; `CTesselationPatch` interpolates the two keys of a moving tessellation. `CDLObject` calls the same ray `transform()`. 41 lines in `src/ri/geometry/*.cpp` read a ray's `time`, plus 3 in `objectMisc.h` and 1 in `render/geometryDispatch.cpp` (`CSurface::intersect`).
- **The copies do not share arithmetic.** The quadrics interpolate their parameters in double (`this->r * (1.0 - rv->time) + nextData[0] * (double)rv->time`, cast to float); `interpolatev` (`mathSpec.h:126`, used by the patch, polygon and tessellation intersections and by `transform()`) also computes in double; the `sample()` paths use `const double ctime`; but `interpolate()` and `interpolate3()` in `objectMisc.h` multiply in float. A single evaluator is therefore bit-identical to the starting tree only if it keeps each call site's arithmetic (FR-014).
- **The same motion is implemented a second time in each family's `sample()`**, for grid and hit shading (`ctime` interpolation of the two keys, per `varying[VARIABLE_TIME]`). Shading calls `sample()` for every hider (`shading.cpp:941,1043,1111,1132,1199`), so the raytrace hider reaches this code as well as Reyes.
- **Camera motion has a third implementation.** `CRendererContext::addObject` clones the object's transform and synthesizes a `next` transform flagged `cameraMotion`, expands the object's bound, and samples the rotation arc at three times (`rendererContext.cpp:690-760`). `transform()` applies the SLERP of the camera's relative rotation (`CRenderer::relRotQ`, `cameraHasRotation`, `toWorld`) only when that flag is set (`objectMisc.h:194`). Reyes applies its own SLERP in `reyes.cpp`, `stochastic.cpp` and `stochasticQuad.h`. `slerpq`/`relRotQ` appear in 10 files.
- **Probed on this tree (2026-10-27; two throwaway scenes, both hiders; the scene files are not part of the spec):** an object that carries its own transform motion (`MotionBegin` around its `Translate`) gets **no camera motion blur**, because `addObject` only adds camera motion when the object's transform has no `next` already (`rendererContext.cpp:692`). A static cylinder in the same scene blurs in both hiders; the moving one does not, in either. A polygon (vertex-keyed) blurs under the same camera translation in raytrace and in Reyes.
- **The raytrace camera is code inside the hider.** `CRaytracer::computeSamples` (`raytracer.cpp:626-700`) builds each primary ray inline: pinhole when `aperture == 0`; otherwise a thin lens that offsets the ray origin by the lens point (`lensU`, `lensV` from spec 039's lens slot, scaled by `CRenderer::aperture`) and aims at the focal plane. It also sets the ray differentials `da`/`db`. It reads seven camera statics (`imagePlane`, `invImagePlane`, `projection`, `focaldistance`, `aperture`, `dxdPixel`, `dydPixel`), each a DB-011 allowlist line (7 of the 44 for `raytracer.cpp`), partly through `fullImagePixels2camera()` (`renderer.h:618`).
- **Reyes has a second depth-of-field model**: a circle of confusion (`CSampler::circleOfConfusion`, `cocFactorSamples`, 7 call sites in `reyes.cpp`), a screen-space scatter rather than a true lens. `sampler.h` documents the difference as spec 008's D9, "a documented non-closable residual". Reyes is out of scope (spec 031).
- **Other camera and frame values Geometry reads from `CRenderer`** are in dicing code shared with Reyes: `object.cpp` and `surface.cpp` hold 16 DB-004 lines (`imagePlane`, `projection`, `dPixeldx`/`dPixeldy`, `pixelLeft`/`pixelTop`, `cocFactorPixels`, `invFocaldistance`, `flags`, `raytracingFlags`), and 6 more lines (`cameraHasRotation`, `relRotQ`, `toWorld` from `dlobject.cpp` and `quadrics.cpp`) come from the ray `transform()`.
- **Architecture check (macOS, isolated build):** `ALLOWED` DB-004 x 65, DB-011 x 173, DB-009 x 0 (DB-009 is "tracked by review", not an edge, so it has no allowlist line). Metrics: Geometry 78 `CRenderer::` references over 19 members (baseline 78/19); Hiders 567 over 84 (baseline 567/84). The registry text says DB-011 has 180 edges per platform; the check reports 173 on macOS, so the registry count is stale.
- **Existing tests in this area** (`ctest -N`): 15 camera/motion/DOF visual scenes (`motion-{1,2,3}-{reyes,raytrace}`, `teapot-motion-*`, `camera-dof-*`, `camera-motion-{small,small+dof}-*`, `camera-motion-huge-reyes`), 9 blobby-motion visual scenes, 10 `Parity_*` tests (`dof`, `dof-motion`, `motion-{patches,polygons,quadrics}-{translate,deform}`, `motion-subdiv-{translate,rotate}`) plus 4 blobby motion parity tests, `Convergence_dof-*` and `Convergence_motion-*`, `CameraSamples_*`, `Sampling_Camera*`, and 57 of the 419 `add_determinism_sweep` scenes (the sweep requires bit-identity across thread counts and configurations, not against master). `test_disk_sampling` (`DiskSampling`) tests `sampleDisk()` in `src/common/random.h`, which the hiders no longer call since spec 039; its relevance is for the plan to check.

### User Story 1 - Every raytrace camera ray comes from one camera layer (Priority: P1)

A hider author, or the author of a later integrator, asks the camera for a primary ray at film position (x, y), shutter time t and lens point (u, v), and gets a ray with its origin, direction, time and differentials. The raytrace hider does exactly that, and no hider carries its own projection or lens code.

**Why this priority**: It is the camera-side half of V6, and 031 (Reyes) and the integrators of 042 and 050 need this seam. The raytrace hider is the first consumer because it is the hider that already has a true lens ray.

**Independent Test**: Unit tests of the camera layer against analytic rays and against a copy of the starting tree's arithmetic, using test doubles; a render comparison of every camera-ray scene against the starting tree.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a perspective frame with no depth of field, **When** the camera layer is asked for the ray at a film position, **Then** the ray starts at the camera origin, aims through the image plane at that position, carries the requested time, and has the same ray differentials as the starting tree's.
2. **Given** an orthographic frame, **When** the same request is made, **Then** the ray starts at the film position and points along the view axis, with the starting tree's differentials.
3. **Given** a frame with a non-zero aperture, **When** the request carries a lens point, **Then** the ray origin is offset by that point scaled by the aperture radius and the ray passes through the same point on the focal plane that the pinhole ray would, as in the starting tree.
4. **Given** a cropped render window, **When** the camera layer is asked for a film position, **Then** it gives the same ray the full-frame render gives for that position (the crop invariance spec 039 established).
5. **Given** the finished tree, **When** a developer reads `src/ri/hiders/raytracer.cpp`, **Then** it contains no projection, image-plane, aperture, focal-distance or pixel-size read: the ray comes from the camera layer.

### User Story 2 - Every primitive takes its motion from one evaluator (Priority: P1)

A developer adding a primitive, or fixing how motion interpolates, finds one Geometry-owned evaluator that answers "what does this object look like at shutter time t" and is used by the primitive's intersection and by the grid and hit sampling that feeds shading. Motion no longer has a separate copy in each primitive family.

**Why this priority**: It is the other half of V6 and the largest one. The 12 intersection entry points and the families' `sample()` each interpolate the same two keys by their own code.

**Independent Test**: Replay tests that record, on the starting tree, the hit (distance, u, v, normal) of fixed rays at several shutter times for each family and replay them on the finished tree; evaluator unit tests with a test double that names no hider.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a moving primitive of each family (quadrics, patches, polygons, implicit surface, tessellation), **When** a ray at time 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1 is intersected, **Then** the result equals the starting tree's recorded result (bit-identical, FR-014).
2. **Given** a static primitive, **When** the evaluator is asked for any time, **Then** it returns the primitive's own data unchanged, and a ray at any time hits the same point.
3. **Given** the evaluator at time 0 and at time 1, **When** a moving primitive is queried, **Then** it returns exactly the first and second key.
4. **Given** the finished tree, **When** a developer reads the 12 intersection entry points and `CDLObject`, **Then** none interpolates its own motion: each asks the evaluator.
5. **Given** a test double that supplies a time and a ray, **When** the evaluator is used from it, **Then** it works with no hider, no `CRenderer` and no shading context in the test's link line.

### User Story 5 - The camera layer is ready for Reyes and for a bidirectional integrator (Priority: P3)

The author of spec 031 finds that the camera layer exposes the lens model (aperture radius, focal distance) from which Reyes can derive its circle of confusion, and the camera and motion interfaces are ones Reyes can adopt without changing them. ### Edge Cases

- **Zero-length shutter** (`Shutter t t`): `invShutterTime` is 1 today (`renderer.cpp:461`), and sampling of motion is cleared when there is no motion blur. The evaluator at any time must give the first key, and `dPdtime` must come out as today.
- **Motion sampling off** (`OPTIONS_FLAGS_SAMPLEMOTION` cleared): rays carry time 0. The evaluator gives the first key; nothing may start interpolating.
- **Ray time outside 0..1**: the starting tree extrapolates linearly (no clamp). The evaluator must give the same values; adding a clamp is a behaviour change (FR-014).
- **Static primitives, procedurals and CSG solids** (`CDelayedObject`, `CDelayedInstance`, `CCSGSolid`, `CDummyObject`) do not move: the evaluator returns them unchanged and no new code path appears for them.
- **A primitive with its own motion under a moving camera**: the starting tree gives it no camera motion (Measured starting point). The evaluator must preserve this exactly (FR-021(b)); the behaviour is filed as GitHub #106 and any fix is a separate spec or an approved later phase.
- **Camera rotation, translation and rotation-only** (`cameraRotationOnly`): all three paths must produce what they do today, including the bound expansion that samples the arc.
- **Two-key motion only**: `RiMotionBegin` with more than two keys is reduced to shutter open and close by extrapolation (`addMotion`, `rendererContext.cpp:835-850`). The evaluator keeps two keys; multi-key motion is out of scope.
- **CSG region predicate under motion**: region predicates and `regionCoverage` classify at shutter open and undisplaced (`csgDefinition.cpp:137`). The evaluator must not change when or where they are evaluated (FR-015, confirmed 2026-10-27).
- **Instances and delayed expansion**: an object inside an instance or a procedural expansion gets its motion data when it is added; the evaluator must read the same data.
- **Hot paths**: `generateRay` runs once per camera sample and the evaluator runs once per ray-primitive test. Neither may add allocation or locking, and neither may add a measurable cost (SC-008); the one new call per camera sample is accepted if SC-008 holds.
- **Threads**: the frame parameters are read by every render thread; they are immutable for the frame and need no lock.
- **Crop and network rendering**: `-P:N` and crop windows must give the same rays for the same absolute film position, as spec 039 requires.
- **Session reuse**: a second `RiBegin`/`RiEnd` in the same process builds fresh frame parameters; nothing leaks across sessions (cf. gotcha 20).
- **Build options**: a build without the JIT, and the `ribVector`, preview, Python and Lua consumers that include Geometry or hider headers, still build.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**Frame parameters**

- **FR-001**: There MUST be an immutable per-frame parameter set, `FrameParams`, built once per frame by the renderer core and read by Hiders and Geometry without any reference to `CRenderer`. It MUST carry at least: shutter open and close and the reciprocal of their difference; the projection type and image plane; the pixel-to-camera scale and the screen window origin; the lens parameters (aperture radius, focal distance, focal length, f-stop); and the camera-motion data (camera-to-world and world-to-camera matrices at shutter open and close, the relative rotation and translation, and the has-motion, has-rotation and rotation-only flags). Values MUST equal the ones `CRenderer` holds today.
- **FR-002**: `FrameParams` MUST NOT duplicate what spec 039's immutable per-frame `CSamplingConfig` already carries to the hiders (the screen window origin among them); the plan decides whether the two are one structure or one refers to the other. It MUST be readable from more than one render thread with no lock.
- **FR-003**: `FrameParams` MUST live where both Geometry and Hiders may depend on it under Principle VIII's allowed-dependency table, and it MUST NOT include `renderer.h` or `rendererContext.h`. The renderer core builds and installs it; no domain pulls it from the core.

**Camera layer**

- **FR-004**: There MUST be a camera interface, `ICamera`, with `generateRay(x, y, t, lensUV)`. It returns the primary ray: origin and direction in camera space, the ray's shutter time, and the ray differentials. `x` and `y` are a position in the full image's pixels, `t` is a shutter fraction in 0..1, and `lensUV` is the unit-disk lens point.
- **FR-005**: `generateRay` MUST consume spec 039's camera sample without redrawing it: `x`, `y` from the film slot through the existing jitter mapping, `t` from the time slot, `lensUV` from the lens slot through the concentric disk map, all as `CSampler::cameraSample` produces them today. The camera layer MUST hold no random state and draw no random number.
- **FR-006**: It MUST support perspective and orthographic projection, a pinhole (aperture zero) and a thin lens, and MUST produce the rays and ray differentials that `CRaytracer::computeSamples` produces today for the same inputs, bit for bit (FR-014). It MUST keep the crop invariance of spec 039: a film position gives the same ray in a cropped and in the full render.
- **FR-007**: The camera layer MUST expose the lens model (aperture radius, focal distance, and the quantities a thin-lens circle of confusion needs) so that spec 031 can derive Reyes's circle of confusion from it. This spec MUST NOT change any Reyes code or the Reyes depth-of-field result.
- **FR-008**: The raytrace hider MUST generate every primary ray through `ICamera` and MUST read no camera static for ray generation or ray differentials. Hiders other than raytrace are unchanged.
- **FR-009**: `ICamera` MUST reserve named room for BDPT camera importance, `evaluateImportance`, and connection sampling, `sampleConnection` (reference doc §7.4), with no behaviour: an implementer that provides only `generateRay` MUST still be valid; the reserved operations MUST report "not supported". Adding their behaviour later MUST NOT change `generateRay` or force an existing implementer to change.
- **FR-010**: The camera layer MUST be testable with a test double that links no hider, no Shading and no renderer core.

**Motion evaluator**

- **FR-011**: There MUST be one Geometry-owned motion evaluator, `CObject::atTime(t)` or its equivalent, that gives a moving object's quantities at shutter fraction `t` from its two motion keys (shutter open at `t = 0`, shutter close at `t = 1`, linear between and beyond, as today). A static object MUST return its own data unchanged. At `t = 0` and `t = 1` it MUST return exactly the first and the second key.
- **FR-012**: The evaluator MUST be hider-agnostic and MUST NOT name a hider, a shading context or `CRenderer`; it takes its frame values from `FrameParams`. It MUST be usable from the raytrace hider now and from Reyes in spec 031 without a second implementation. Constitution Principle VIII rule 3 and the maintainer's standing rule on effects apply: motion blur is implemented once, generically.
- **FR-013**: Every primitive's intersection MUST move onto the evaluator, one primitive family per phase: the seven quadrics, the bilinear patch, the polygon triangle and quad, the implicit surface, the tessellation patch, and the ray transform that `CDLObject` shares with the quadrics. After each phase the suite MUST be green. When the last phase lands, none of the 12 intersection entry points (nor `CDLObject`) interpolates its own motion.
- **FR-014**: **Behaviour preservation (byte-identical).** Every relocation phase MUST render byte-identically to the starting tree (master) on every image of the suite from phase 4 on, camera, motion, DOF and blobby-motion scenes included, for the raytrace, Reyes and zbuffer hiders (phase 3 changes no consumer and runs the same comparison). Each call site keeps its own arithmetic: a single evaluator serving the intersection and `sample()` paths exposes per-site precision (for example a float entry point and a double entry point) because the copies do not share it (Measured starting point). The check is a starting-tree before/after comparison: an image manifest of the rendered scenes and a MaxBlockAvgDiff table, in the pattern of spec 025's `image_manifest.sh` and `affected-*.tsv` runs, taken on the starting tree before any code moves and compared after each phase. The determinism sweep cannot judge this: it only checks consistency across thread counts and configurations, never change relative to master, so it is a separate guard and not evidence of byte-identity. Any intended output change (unifying the interpolation precision, a different DOF treatment, a fix to the chord-versus-arc interpolation of gotcha 7) MUST land in its own later phase, with the changed scenes measured and listed, and a reference re-render only on the maintainer's explicit approval at that time. No threshold is ever widened and no existing test assertion is weakened. Rendering MUST also stay bit-identical across the determinism sweep's configurations.
- **FR-015**: The evaluator MUST NOT change when or where the CSG region predicate and `regionCoverage` classify: they stay at shutter open and undisplaced, and a solid's operands are classified as today (confirmed by the maintainer 2026-10-27): the evaluator never feeds them an interpolated position.

**Grid sampling and the shutter statics**

- **FR-016**: Removing every Geometry read of `CRenderer::invShutterTime` requires the 24 `dPdtime` reads in the families' `sample()` to obtain the shutter scale from `FrameParams`, or from the evaluator, instead. After this spec no file under `src/ri/geometry` or `src/ri/hiders` MAY name `CRenderer::invShutterTime`. The 7 DB-004 `invShutterTime` allowlist lines MUST be deleted.
- **FR-017**: The Shading-side reads of `invShutterTime` (`rendererServices.h`, `shading.cpp`) reach the value through the services interface and are not allowlist lines. They MUST keep working as today and MUST NOT be edited by this spec beyond what its own tests require, to avoid overlap with spec 027 (which edits `shading.cpp` and `rendererServicesImpl.h` for textures). The renderer core MAY keep the static for them. The `time` shading-variable conversion at `shading.cpp:269,408` is out of scope (GitHub #107).
- **FR-018**: **The grid-side boundary.** 028 moves both the intersection and the Geometry-internal grid-sampling time handling (each family's `sample()`/`interpolate()` time interpolation and `dPdtime`) onto the evaluator, keeping per-site precision (FR-014). The sampling path is shared by both hiders' shading and is where the 24 `invShutterTime` reads are. Spec 031 owns the Reyes hider code (`reyes.cpp`, `stochastic*.h`, `zbuffer*`), the dicing probes in `surface.cpp`/`object.cpp` (`timev = 1`, the moving-grid probes) and the circle of confusion; 028 changes none of them.

**Camera motion**

- **FR-019**: The ray-side camera-motion transform (`transform()` in `objectMisc.h`, including its SLERP of the relative rotation under `cameraHasRotation`) MUST be reached through the evaluator and `FrameParams`, so that the quadrics' and `CDLObject`'s reads of `cameraHasRotation`, `relRotQ` and `toWorld` (6 DB-004 lines) go. Its results MUST be those of the starting tree (FR-014).
- **FR-020**: `CRendererContext::addObject`'s synthesis of a camera-motion transform and its bound expansion MAY remain in the renderer core for this spec, built on `FrameParams`; it is shared with Reyes. The plan decides.
- **FR-021**: **Camera rotation and the starting tree's gap.** (a) The camera-rotation SLERP (gotcha 7, `cameraHasRotation`, `relRotQ`) moves behind the evaluator for the raytrace side only. Reyes's own copies in `reyes.cpp`, `stochastic.cpp` and `stochasticQuad.h` stay until spec 031, so for one spec the SLERP exists in two places; an equivalence test between the evaluator's SLERP and Reyes's MUST pin them to the same results. (b) An object that carries its own transform motion MUST keep receiving no camera motion blur, exactly as today in both hiders: the evaluator composes only what `addObject` composes today. The behaviour is filed as GitHub #106; fixing it is not in 028 and would need its own approved phase under FR-014.

### Key Entities

- **`FrameParams`**: the immutable per-frame set of shutter, raster, lens and camera-motion values, built by the renderer core and read by Hiders and Geometry.
- **`ICamera`**: the interface that turns a camera sample (x, y, t, lensUV) into a primary ray, exposes the lens model, and reserves camera importance and connection sampling for a bidirectional integrator.
- **Camera sample**: spec 039's film, lens and time values for one subpixel, produced by the sampler, consumed by `ICamera`.
- **Motion evaluator** (`atTime`): Geometry's single answer to "this object at shutter fraction t", from its two keys.
- **Motion keys**: the object's two states, at shutter open (t = 0) and shutter close (t = 1).
- **Primitive families**: quadrics (7), patches, polygons (triangle and quad), implicit surface, tessellation patch, and the `CDLObject` ray transform; each migrates in its own phase.
- **Camera motion transform**: the synthesized shutter-close transform and the relative-rotation SLERP that carry camera motion to each object.
