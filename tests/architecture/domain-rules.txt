# Infra may depend on nothing, or archcheck exits 2.
#
# Source: .specify/memory/constitution.md v1.5.0, Principle VIII,
# "Allowed dependencies" and "Utility libraries". Core is the composition root; Consumer covers
# executables, tools, bindings and preview (research R6); Infra is the shared
# floor every class may use (research R5). The plug-in domains (ShaderDSO,
# Procedures, Display) may use only their own ABI header, which is part of
# their own class, plus Infra. ImageIO is the Image I/O utility library (spec 048):
# it depends on Infra only, and only Texturing and Display (plus Core and Consumer)
# may depend on it.
